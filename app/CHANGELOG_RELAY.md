# Relay 0.1.0

Relay's first intentional early release.

## Highlights

- A new Relay identity and visual language across Android and Linux.
- Local-network file and folder sharing between Android and Linux devices.
- A unified nearby-device sharing surface with clear transfer progress and cancellation.
- Persistent Relay device identity and authenticated Relay peers.
- Redesigned Home, Settings, About, selection, and dialog experiences for desktop and Android.
